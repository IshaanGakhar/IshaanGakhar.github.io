I’m Ishaan Gakhar, an AI researcher pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at Stony Brook University, expected in 2028. I completed my B.Tech. in Information Technology at Manipal Institute of Technology in 2026. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, efficient deep learning, generative models, and multimodal AI, with a strong focus on multiscale learning, segmentation, super-resolution, federated learning, and knowledge-driven document understanding.

My recent work spans Laplacian pyramid-based methods for image enhancement and segmentation, multimodal generation, privacy-aware learning, and legal AI for document understanding and structured knowledge extraction. I’m particularly interested in building models that are efficient, robust, and useful in real-world settings.

## Current education and research interests
